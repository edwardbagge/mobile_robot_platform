This folder includes tests related to wheel encoders on the ESP32.

Goals:

    1) Confirm both encoders are wired correctly
    2) Confirm tick counts increase and decrease consistently
    3) Confirm left/right direction signs are correct
    4) Confirm counts are stable at low and moderate wheel speed
    5) Prepare encoder feedback for later RPI 5 / ROS 2 integration

Suggested test order:

    1) Spin each wheel by hand and observe raw tick counts
    2) Run the motors slowly and confirm counts change smoothly
    3) Verify left and right encoders report expected direction
    4) Compare equal-time wheel motion between left and right sides
    5) Extend serial output into a structured message format later
