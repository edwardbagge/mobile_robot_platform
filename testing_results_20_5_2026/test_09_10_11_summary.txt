TEST SUMMARY
Date reviewed: 2026-05-20

test_09
Result: SUCCESS
Purpose:
- ROS 2 bridge from /robot_command string messages to ESP32 serial motor commands.

What worked:
- Package built successfully.
- Serial port /dev/ttyUSB0 opened successfully.
- Commands s, f, x, and z were sent from ROS 2 and received by the ESP32.
- ESP32 reported status correctly.
- Forward command produced encoder feedback.
- Motor timeout stop worked.
- Encoder reset worked.

Notes:
- ESP32 reboot text appeared at startup, but this was expected because the ESP32 reset button was pressed physically at the start of the test.
- During forward motion, left ticks were positive and right ticks were negative. This may be normal depending on the encoder sign convention.


test_10
Result: PARTIAL SUCCESS
Purpose:
- ROS 2 bridge from /cmd_vel Twist messages to ESP32 serial motor commands.

What worked:
- Serial port /dev/ttyUSB0 opened successfully.
- /cmd_vel commands were mapped and sent correctly:
  - forward -> f
  - backward -> b
  - left turn -> l
  - right turn -> r
  - stop -> x
- ESP32 received each command and reported the matching motor action.

Issues observed:
- Forward motion looked plausible and both encoder values changed.
- Backward motion was suspicious: left encoder changed significantly, but right encoder stayed almost fixed near -1160.
- Right turn was also suspicious: left encoder changed significantly, but right encoder stayed almost fixed near -2330.

Conclusion:
- The /cmd_vel to serial bridge worked.
- The test did not cleanly validate balanced drivetrain or right encoder behavior across all motions.


test_11
Result: PARTIAL SUCCESS
Purpose:
- Combined /cmd_vel to ESP32 serial bridge plus parsing and publishing encoder feedback to ROS topics.

What worked:
- Serial port /dev/ttyUSB0 opened successfully.
- The node sent the expected motor commands from /cmd_vel input.
- Encoder feedback lines from the ESP32 were parsed successfully.
- Parsed values were published to:
  - /left_encoder_ticks
  - /right_encoder_ticks
- Topic echoes matched the parsed values from the general log.

Issues observed:
- The right encoder showed little or no change during backward motion while the left encoder changed substantially.
- The right encoder also changed very little during right-turn motion while the left encoder changed substantially.

Conclusion:
- ROS integration worked: command bridging, serial parsing, and encoder topic publishing were successful.
- The test does not show clean, correct encoder behavior for both wheels under all commanded motions.


Overall summary
- test_09: PASS
- test_10: PASS for bridge functionality, but FAIL as a clean drivetrain/encoder validation
- test_11: PASS for ROS integration and encoder topic publishing, but FAIL as a clean two-wheel encoder validation
